## Concept Scope

The age-policy-framework repository presents an age-aware AI risk policy framework. Its purpose is to help guide ethical AI interaction boundaries by developmental stage, especially for minors and other users who may need additional protection from cognitive dependency, over-automation, or manipulative engagement design.

The intended purpose is:

- age-stratified AI risk governance;
- prevention of cognitive dependency in minors;
- reflective and exploratory learning support;
- education-centric design over engagement maximization;
- safer AI interaction depth based on developmental context.

## Boundary

This framework should not be used to deny children's dignity, autonomy, education, or appropriate access to support.

It is not a medical, psychological, or legal determination tool.

Any AI-assisted editing, documentation, analysis, or implementation support is assistance only and does not transfer authorship or ownership away from LORI-FRAMEWORK.
